Living Well: Work-Life Balance

Medicine is a meaningful calling - but caring for others should not mean losing ourselves. Beyond the clinic are families to cherish, passions to rediscover, journeys to take, and a life to enjoy.

Can excellence and balance grow together? Join Thiru V. Irai Anbu for an inspiring conversation on purpose, relationships, resilience, and the art of living well.

Pause. Listen. Reflect. Return with practical ideas to protect your energy, make space for what matters, and build a career that is successful, balanced, and joyful. Step away from the rush and rediscover life beyond medicine.

The Purpose..

Looking Beyond Medicine is a vibrant monthly forum for doctors ready to think wider, lead better, and live more purposefully. Each gathering brings an accomplished voice from outside the clinical world to spark fresh thinking.

Expect practical insights, candid conversations, shared experiences, and meaningful connections with peers. Here, new perspectives become confident decisions, stronger teams, and more fulfilling careers.
